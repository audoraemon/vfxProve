What I changed as the designer

  • One focus. The wheel is the only bright thing. Neighbours are pushed back (smaller, dimmer) until you hover them.
  • Colour means one thing. Each Authority has its own hue (Dominion violet, Ruin ember, Death green). Gold always means "yours or buyable": learned rings, your points, the Raise button.
  • Your tier ring glows. Rings you can't reach are dashed, with a lock. Tier numerals I–V sit on the rings.
  • Nodes are icon only. A node shows its icon, short name and level ticks. Every number lives in the bottom panel.
  • The panel reads left to right: see it (the clip) → what it is → what the next level gives → act. Remembrance is cool blue (a memory, free). Raise is solid gold (spending).
  • The preview clip is the power's existing draft clip (152×86, 16 frames), so every power already has one.
