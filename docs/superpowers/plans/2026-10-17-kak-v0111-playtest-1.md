## Review Focus

1. **Keys around the legend:** Esc with the legend open closes it and does not open the pause menu; H and TAB during the ending do nothing; H during the intro freezes the tour too; a second freeze never stacks. Test in Task 3 (`_keys_while_open`).
2. **Restart or a new act with the legend open:** R is ignored while open; `next_act()` / `start()` closes the panel and unfreezes. Test in Task 3 (`_closes_on_start`).
3. **A soldier held while on someone's errand:** a soldier whispered, confused or lured while on a director's DUTY, at his post during the rally, or as the town evacuates ends at its post (`Mind.POST`), never idle in a citizen's RECOVER/CALM, never fleeing. Test in Task 5 (`_soldier_comes_back`).
4. **The counting-house burns at an awkward moment:** while a collector visits a debtor, flees, hides, or after one is safe; a fall after burning. One banner, each quarry resolves once, none left WAITING, its strip rows gone. Test in Task 4 (`_flush_awkward`).
5. **The collapsed HUD carries across:** TAB state survives R and an act change; the hint flashes once at each act's play start; a ticking `{n}` never re-flashes. Test in Task 1 (`_flash_rules`).
