> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. **Goal:** Answer the user's second playtest: every power open on every mission; a mix you can hear (lower music, three volume sliders, a death sound on every kill, a cue of its own for every quiet power, measured); a strike whose danger counts from its impact with a startle before the run; and a bell whose toll never loses outright -- The Lantern Turns (dawn x1.5 / x2.0, a Lantern-Bearer to kill, a Herald to stop on the bell nights).

**Architecture:** Pools are data (emptied). The clock's rate is one Rules field read by the clock, `EventTimeline` and the Lantern nights' schedules. Awareness is a held-danger queue in `Crowd` keyed by `PowerBook.IMPACT` plus a deterministic startle in `Person.panic()`. The Lantern is one shared `LanternWatch` (on Rules) with a `LanternBearer`, Herald runs (WarningDirector's goal switch and `TempleReport`), and a `MissionDef.lantern` kind each night adopts. Sound is buses + `Settings` + `OptionsPanel`, a pure `DeathVoices`, foley recipes, and a loudness table the suite reads.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2 GDScript; headless suite `tests/run_all.gd` / `tools/dev/run_suite_parallel.sh`; references `tools/dev/refs_check.sh`; scripted play `tools/dev/behaviour_check.gd` (+ `tools/dev/routes/tier*_b.gd`); gates `tools/dev/gate.sh`; landing `tools/dev/land.sh`; sound `tools/audio/foley.py` (+ new `tools/audio/loudness.py`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-kak-v0112-playtest-2.md` (Decisions 1-33; cited D1-D33). Rulings: `.superpowers/sdd/v0112/rulings.md` (R1-R41; binding over this text where they differ).

## Review Focus

1. **The held danger and the act's end:** a Heaven cast 0.2 s before an act ends (R, `next_act()`, the ending) frightens no one in the next act; `Crowd.clear()` drops held dangers. Test in Task 4 (`_held_dies_with_act`).
2. **The startle never shifts a random stream:** `startle_seconds()` reads `rng.seed`, draws nothing; two panics of one person give one value. Test in Task 4 (`_startle_deterministic`).
3. **Death sounds never touch the simulation:** after a 60-body kill, `crowd._rng.state` and every person's `rng.state` are what they were without `DeathVoices`. Test in Task 8 (`_no_draws`).
4. **Mission time vs real time:** at rate 2.0 the clock and the timeline run double, cooldowns and a person's walk do not; Abolition's clock law still stops the clock. Test in Task 2 (`_rates`).
5. **The Lantern latches:** a second toll after The Bell Lies' unring changes nothing; Blind is final; a Bearer killed during the hit-stop of the cast that also burns the Lantern ends Blind (the kill wins). Test in Task 7 (`_latches`) and Task 10 (`_burn_and_kill_same_cast`).
6. **A Herald's warning:** killed unseen, the warning dies and the objective counts it; seen, the witness carries it on to the Temple; a later star after the toll runs straight for the Temple. Test in Task 11 (`_herald_relay`).
