# KAK v0.10 — The Lantern campaign — design

**Baseline:** `kak-v0.09`, branch `feat/Develop-Main`.

**Goal:** give KAK a story and a campaign. A forgotten god wakes over Aldermere, weak, and must regain its strength before Halcyon, the god of the Church, finds it and eats it. The campaign is a few nights in one town. Each night is a mission. How the player regains strength (converting believers, stealing Halcyon's power, or destroying his anchors) decides what kind of god they become and how the campaign ends.

**This is a prototype.** It covers one town and four nights. The full campaign (more Tiers, the holy city and the capital) comes later, once this is fun.

## Decisions (with the user, 2026-10-05)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Story or missions first | A one-page lore spine first; mission details grow out of play |
| The rival | **Halcyon, the Lantern**, the god of the Church, protects humanity in this era. He is distant, not evil: he feeds on faith and lets corrupt priests rule in his name |
| The threat | Halcyon eats lesser gods. Each failed night he takes a **bite**; three bites and the god is gone |
| The awakening | **The oracle's sacrifice.** Brother Cael, the Church's oracle, foresaw the god's waking and gave his life alone in a ritual to bring it about |
| Why Cael turned heretic | The Church **burned his lover**, Mira, a healer who secretly kept the god's last shrine |
| Memory | Mira's prayers were the only voice that reached the sleeping god. Cael's soul went into the god with his sacrifice, so the god wakes with his memories |
| The player's god | **Forgotten, shaped by play.** Its nature is lost; the path the player takes decides what it becomes |
| Cult at the start | **None.** Followers are made during play |
| Paths | **Three ways to regain strength:** Faith (convert believers), Theft (steal Halcyon's gifts), Ruin (destroy his anchors). One shared strength (DP), plus a path tally that decides title and ending |
| Setting | **One town, many nights** (Aldermere) for the prototype. Later Tiers move to the holy city and the capital |
| The Long Night in the campaign | **Only its middle act** (The Festival or The Procession) is Night 3. The Long Night stays on the mission board unchanged |
| Night 2 set-piece | **Halcyon's Searchlight** in The Vigil Flame |
| Names | Halcyon, Mira, Cael, Odran, Venn, Wren, Bram and Hollis are kept for now |
| Version | **v0.10.** It takes the campaign save planned for v0.10; Resonance and Awakening Trials move later |

## 1. Lore spine

**The world.** Before the Church came, the people of this land prayed to many small gods. One of them was the player's god. Its name has been forgotten for centuries.

**Halcyon, the Lantern.** The god of the Church. He takes in his people's faith and gives back protection: good harvests, plagues that stay away, walls that hold. He never speaks. His priests speak for him and rule Aldermere through the Temple. The Feast of Lanterns is his festival, and every lantern lit is a prayer to him. He is not cruel. He simply never looks at what is done in his name.

**Mira.** A healer who lived in the west quarter. Her grandmother taught her the old name, and she kept the god's last broken shrine at the forest edge. The Church called her a witch, and Archprelate Odran signed the order. Sister Venn lit her pyre in the market square.

**Brother Cael.** The Temple's oracle and Mira's secret lover. His visions served the Church for years. After the burning he had one last vision: a forgotten god waking over Aldermere, and his own death as the price. He took up Mira's faith, climbed to her shrine at dusk, and gave his life to finish the prayer she never could.

**The awakening.** The sacrifice pulls the god out of the dark as a star falling over the Main Gate. The god is weak and remembers nothing of itself. It knows Mira's voice from her prayers, and it carries Cael's soul and memories.

**The danger.** If the town cries out to Halcyon (the bell, mass prayer), he turns his gaze on Aldermere, finds the god weak and eats part of it. This is why the god must stay hidden while it is weak, and why its first night is about stopping a warning.

**The hidden truth.** Cael's memories are biased. His vision named "a heretic who keeps the old shrine", and when Odran asked who, Cael told him. His sacrifice is atonement as much as faith. The player learns this in the last memory fragment.

**The question the player carries:** Halcyon let this happen. Is the god any better?

## 3. Strength, bites and paths

### 3.1 Divinity: the DP budget grows

- The campaign starts at **3 slots and 6 DP**.
- **Winning a night:** +2 DP. **A bonus objective:** +1 DP. At most one bonus counts per night.
- **Slots by night:** 3, 3, 4, 6.
- DP is a loadout budget, as since v0.08. Nothing is spent during a mission.
- A run that wins every night has 6, 8, 10 and 12 DP (up to 14 with every bonus), in line with The Long Night's and Last Judgement's 14 DP.

### 3.2 Bites

- **Losing a night** is a bite: **−1 DP for the rest of the campaign**. The campaign goes on to the next night (fail forward), except as below.
- **Three bites:** Halcyon has eaten the god. The campaign is over and restarts from Night 1.
- **Losing the finale** is a bite, and the finale is played again until it is won or the god is eaten.
- The DP budget never falls below **4 DP**.

### 3.3 Paths

| Path | Title | Strength from | Authorities |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Faith** | The Prophet's God | Converting the Grieving into Believers | Dominion |
| **Theft** | The Deceiver | Stealing what Halcyon gave the town; making prayers to him feed the god instead | Veil, Disorder |
| **Ruin** | The Kataclysm | Destroying Halcyon's anchors and draining their power | Ruin, Life/Death |

- **Path tally:** each night that belongs to a path adds 1 to that path when played, won or lost.
- **In the prototype only Night 2 belongs to a path**, so the path is the Night 2 mission chosen. Night 3 gives DP only. The tally is kept as a count so later Tiers can add path nights; a tie goes to the most recent path night.
- **The path decides:**
  - the **title** shown on the campaign and results screens;
  - **what follows Night 3:** the Ruin path plays Night 4 (Last Judgement); Faith and Theft go to their ending after Night 3;
  - **the ending** (§5.4).

### 3.4 Halcyon's Gaze

- A HUD meter, 0–100, in the three Night 2 missions only. The Warning, The Long Night's acts and Last Judgement keep their own rules and show no Gaze.
- **A full Gaze loses the night:** banner "THE LANTERN LOOKS".
- The Gaze never falls during a night.
- Sources (starting values, tuned in balance):

| Source | Gaze |
|---|---|
| A death someone sees | +10 |
| Each Faithful praying (in a beam, at a shrine) | +1 per second each, at most +6 per second in total |
| A report reaching the Temple | fills it |
| The bell ringing | fills it |
| The searchlight touching Wren | +50 |

## 4. The campaign: four nights in Aldermere

| Night | Tier | Slots | Mission | Memory fragment before it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 3 | **The Warning** (unchanged) | F1 *The Shrine* |
| 2 | 2 | 3 | **Choice card:** Mira's House (Faith), The Vigil Flame (Theft) or Broken Lanterns (Ruin) | F2 *The Pyre* |
| 3 | 3 | 4 | **The Long Night's middle act:** its choice card between The Festival and The Procession (unchanged acts) | F3 *The Lanterns* |
| 4 | 5 | 6 | **Last Judgement** (unchanged), Ruin path only | F4 *The Vision* |

- Every night uses the campaign's current DP budget (§3.1).
- **Faith and Theft paths:** after Night 3, F4 plays, then the path's ending screen.
- **Each night starts a fresh Aldermere.** Days pass between nights. Ruins and the dead do not carry over in the prototype; only the campaign state does.
- **What carries over:** the DP budget, bites, the path tally, and Night 1's result. If the bell rang in Night 1, Night 3's act starts on The Long Night's "bell rang" row (Organized; soldiers watch the square), through `NightState.bell_rang`.

### 4.1 Night 2 — the Vigil

All three missions take place on the night of the **Vigil**. A priest, the **flame-bearer**, carries the Temple's eternal flame in a silver lantern around the town to relight Halcyon's **six wayside shrines**, walking with two acolytes. The Vigil walks the same route in all three missions and is a moving obstacle in each.

- **Town profile:** Unaware, as in The Warning.
- **The Faithful:** priests, the acolytes and about 20 devout citizens (a new citizen flag). A Faithful who sees something forbidden runs to the Temple to report it. A report passes to a witness if its carrier is killed in front of one. This reuses the messenger-and-relay logic of `WarningDirector`.
- **Power pools:**
  - Mira's House and The Vigil Flame: Mind Whisper, Silent Doom, Will-o'-Wisp, Discord, Thornwall.
  - Broken Lanterns: those five, plus Heaven Splitter, Tornado Tempest, Dragonfire Parade and Gravity Distortion.

#### Faith — Mira's House (2:30)

**Premise.** Mira's house in the west quarter has been shut since the burning. Inside are her hidden shrine and Cael's journal of his last vision, which proves the god is real. Lead the right people there without the Faithful noticing.

- **The Grieving:** 10 citizens with a faint marker (people Mira healed, or whose families the Church burned). Only they can be converted.
- **Conversion:**
  1. A Grieving citizen enters the house: Mind Whisper drags them there, or Will-o'-Wisp lures them.
  2. They stay inside 10 s, reading.
  3. They become a **Believer** (a new look: a faint ember above the head).
  4. If a Faithful sees a Grieving citizen enter the house, or sees a Believer leave it, that Faithful starts a report. A conversion under way when its reader is seen entering fails.
- **Timed events:**

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0:40 | **The Inquisitor.** Sister Venn searches the west quarter house by house, moving towards Mira's door. If she enters while anyone is inside, a report starts at once |
| 1:15 | **A loud convert.** The newest Believer runs into the street shouting. Within 15 s, whisper them back into the house or silence them; otherwise any Faithful within hearing starts a report |
| 1:45 | **The procession passes.** The Vigil walks the west street for 30 s, with Faithful lining it |
| 2:00 | **The house burns.** Priests set it alight. Anyone inside when the roof falls at 2:30 dies |

- **Win:** at 2:30, at least **5 Believers** are alive and outside the house.
- **Lose:** the Gaze fills (a report reaching the Temple fills it); or 2:30 comes with fewer than 5.
- **Bonus "Pure faith":** nobody dies.
- **Bonus "The journal":** the last Believer to finish reading carries the journal; it is saved if they are alive and outside at 2:30.

#### Theft — The Vigil Flame (3:00)

**Premise.** Halcyon's eternal flame holds part of the power he gave the town. A god cannot hold it: a mortal hand must take it.

**Phase 1 — the swap.**
- The bearer walks the Vigil route with two acolytes.
- **Wren**, a street urchin, appears at 0:50 and watches the silver lantern.
- **The swap:** whisper Wren to the bearer while no acolyte or Faithful can see (the Silent Doom witness rule). It takes 3 s. Wren takes the real flame and leaves a false one.
- If Wren has not been whispered within 30 s of appearing, he tries the swap himself, wherever the bearer is.
- **1:30 — The route shortens:** a suspicious priest sends the procession straight back to the Temple.
- A seen swap starts a report (which fills the Gaze if it reaches the Temple). Phase 2 still begins.
- **Lose in this phase:** the bearer reaches the Temple with the real flame.

**Phase 2 — Halcyon's Searchlight (from the swap until Wren reaches the shrine).**
- The moment the real flame leaves the lantern, the Temple spire flares and a cone of gold light starts sweeping the town.
- **Beams:**
  - one beam at first, on a slow, predictable arc;
  - a **second beam** 30 s after the swap;
  - in the last 20 s of the clock, a beam **stops to search** at the spot of the latest noise (a cast, a death, Discord).
- **Wren's goal:** carry the flame from wherever he made the swap to **Mira's shrine** at the west forest edge.
- **Seen:** a beam touching Wren adds +50 Gaze. Two touches fill it.
- **Faithful in a beam** stop and pray, filling the Gaze (§3.4).
- **Tools:** Mind Whisper steers Wren; Discord makes noise that pulls a searching beam; Will-o'-Wisp is a decoy that a beam follows for 5 s; Thornwall keeps Faithful off his route.
- **Win:** the flame reaches the shrine. The shrine relights with the god's own flame, and the beams die.
- **Lose:** the Gaze fills, Wren dies, or the clock ends.
- **Bonus "Unseen hands":** no beam ever touches Wren.
- **Look:** the existing anticipation dim darkens the town; the cones are additive gold with drifting dust; the spire glows like a lighthouse; building faces in a beam light up through `LightField`.

#### Ruin — Broken Lanterns (3:00)

**Premise.** Halcyon's six wayside shrines anchor his protection over the town. Break them before the Vigil relights them, and drink what was in them.

- **The shrines:** a new structure kind (a stone post with a lantern niche), six of them on the Vigil route.
- **Draining:** a broken shrine is **drained** once it has stayed broken for 20 s. If the bearer reaches a broken shrine before it is drained, he relights it and it stands again. A drained shrine cannot be relit.
- **Each shrine broken:** 3 Faithful run to each standing shrine and pray there (§3.4).
- **1:30 — The Lantern Knights:** 4 elite soldiers (a soldier variant with three times the health) come from the Temple and guard the standing shrines.
- **The last shrine:** when five are drained, 15 Faithful kneel around the sixth and pray. Strike through them (faster, but more Gaze from prayer and witnessed deaths) or draw them away first (safer, slower).
- **Win:** all six shrines drained.
- **Lose:** the bell rings; the Gaze fills; or the clock ends.
- **Bonus "Through the faithful":** the last shrine breaks with at least 10 of its kneelers still within 2 units of it.

### 4.2 Night 3 — the Feast of Lanterns

- The Long Night's choice card offers **The Festival** or **The Procession**, played as single acts with their current rules, timed events and bonuses.
- Their town is set by The Long Night's Act I rows, with Night 1's result standing in for Act I.
- The campaign gives 4 slots and its current budget instead of The Long Night's 14 DP. Balance must check that both acts can be won at 10 DP.

### 4.3 Night 4 — Last Judgement (Ruin path)

Last Judgement as it plays today, with 6 slots and the campaign budget. Balance must check it can be won at 12 DP.

## 5. How the story is told

All text, no voice acting.

### 5.1 Memory fragments

On the interlude screen before each night: Cael's narration over a still panel, 20–40 s, skippable. Prototype panel: a darkened view of the town, plus a PixelLab portrait or panel per fragment where available.

- **F1 The Shrine** (before Night 1): "I climbed to her shrine with the dusk bells behind me. Mira kept it for a god no one remembered. I never learned your name. She knew it. Take what's left of me. Wake."
- **F2 The Pyre** (before Night 2): "Odran read the order. Venn lit the wood. I stood on the Temple steps and said nothing. She looked for me in the crowd. Tonight they carry his flame through the streets as if nothing happened."
- **F3 The Lanterns** (before Night 3): "She hated the Feast. Every lantern is a prayer to him, she said, and he never looks at who lights them. Tomorrow the whole town lights one."
- **F4 The Vision** (before Night 4, or before the Faith and Theft endings): "My vision said a heretic kept the old shrine. Odran asked me who. I told him. That is why I gave you my life. Not faith. Debt."

### 5.2 Cael's lines during missions

Short subtitles on the existing banner system:

| Mission | Event | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Mira's House | Venn arrives | "Venn. She lit Mira's pyre." |
| Mira's House | The house burns | "They're burning her again. Get them out." |
| The Vigil Flame | Wren appears | "The boy wants that lantern. Let him have it." |
| The Vigil Flame | The searchlight wakes | "He's looking. Don't let him see the boy." |
| Broken Lanterns | First shrine drained | "Feel that? That was his." |
| Broken Lanterns | The Knights come | "Odran's knights. He's frightened." |

### 5.3 Choice cards

Each Night 2 card shows the mission's goal and a line from Cael:
- **Faith:** "She'd want them to know you. Let them find her words."
- **Theft:** "He gave this town his light. Take it back."
- **Ruin:** "Break his lanterns. Let him feel how small his town is."

### 5.4 Endings

A title, one panel and a short epilogue in Cael's voice.

- **The New Faith** (Faith): "They pray at her shrine now, quietly, in the dark. They don't know your name either. They call you hers." *Ending screen only in the prototype; a playable final mission comes later.*
- **The False Lantern** (Theft): "The lanterns still burn and they still pray to him. But it's you who hears them now, and he hasn't noticed yet." *Ending screen only in the prototype.*
- **The Kataclysm** (Ruin, once Night 4 is won): "There's no one left to light a lantern. He's starving. So are you. Was this what she prayed for?"
- **Eaten** (three bites): "He found us. I'm sorry, Mira."

## 6. Architecture

New code lives under `src/game/campaign/`, beside `src/game/mission/`.

| Unit | Responsibility | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| `CampaignDef` | The nights in order. Each night is a mission id, a choice of mission ids, or one act of a mission (Night 3); with its Tier, slots, fragment id and optional path | `MissionBook` |
| `CampaignState` | Current night, DP budget, bites, path tally, Night 1's bell result, nights won; the title and ending from the tally | — |
| Save | A `[campaign]` section in `user://kak_save.cfg` holding the state, so a campaign can be continued | `save_file.gd` |
| Flow | Title gains **Campaign** (Continue / New). Campaign → interlude (fragment, then a choice card if the night has one) → Prepare (the night's pool, slots and budget) → Mission → Results → the next night, or an ending | `interlude_screen.gd`, Prepare, Results |
| `GazeMeter` | The 0–100 meter, its sources and its HUD bar; signals when full | Crowd witness events, directors |
| `VigilRoute` | The bearer and acolytes walking the six-shrine route; relighting | `ProcessionDirector`'s walk-and-escort pattern |
| `MirasHouseDirector` | The Grieving, conversion, Venn, the loud convert, the procession, the fire | `WarningDirector` relay, `EventTimeline`, `VigilRoute` |
| `VigilFlameDirector` | Wren, the swap, the route change, phase 2 | `VigilRoute`, `Searchlight` |
| `Searchlight` | The beam cones: arcs, the second beam, the search stop; a `touches(pos)` test; drawing and light pools | `LightField`, impact dim |
| `BrokenLanternsDirector` | Shrine guards, the Knights, the kneelers | `VigilRoute`, Crowd |
| Objectives | `BelieversObjective`, `FlameObjective`, `ShrinesObjective`, `GazeObjective` (fails when full) | `Objective` |
| Structures | The wayside shrine kind; Mira's house as a marked house that can burn | Environment |
| People | `FAITHFUL` and `GRIEVING` citizen flags; the `BELIEVER` look; Venn (`INQUISITOR`), Wren (`URCHIN`), the Lantern Knight (a soldier variant). Enum values are appended last, as always | Crowd |

- **Night 3 as one act:** `CampaignDef` can name a mission and an act. The campaign builds a `NightState` with `bell_rang` from Night 1 and the path from Night 3's choice card, then plays that act alone. The Long Night on the board is untouched.
- **The story text** lives in one data file, `campaign_text.gd` (fragments, lines, card lines, epilogues), so writing changes never touch logic.

## 7. Measures

- **Gates that must not move:** The Warning, The Long Night and Last Judgement played from the board stay unchanged: tests, digest, crowd_check, the exact behaviour checksums, FLOW, the Warning cases, the night scenarios.
- **New tests:**
  - `CampaignState`: DP growth, the one-bonus rule, bites, the 4 DP floor, three bites ending it, the finale replaying, the path tally and ties;
  - the save: write, continue, new;
  - `GazeMeter`: each source and the prayer cap; full loses;
  - Mira's House: conversion timing, a seen entry, Venn's search, the loud-convert window, the fire;
  - The Vigil Flame: the swap witness rule, Wren's own attempt, the route change, beam timing, `touches()`, two touches fill the Gaze, the win at the shrine;
  - Broken Lanterns: draining after 20 s, relighting, guards, the Knights, the kneelers, the bonus;
  - Night 3 as one act with Night 1's bell result.
- **Behaviour scenarios:** `campaign`, with forced outcomes per night (`--n1=win|lose`, `--n2=faith|theft|ruin`, `--n2result=win|lose`, …) so every branch and ending can be reached; one scenario per Night 2 mission reporting its result, time and Gaze.
- **FLOW** covers one campaign from Title through a fragment, a choice card and one night.
- **Balance:** each Night 2 mission winnable at 8 DP by a scripted policy; Night 3's acts at 10 DP; Last Judgement at 12 DP.
- **Bench:** the searchlight with two beams over the full town stays within 5 fps of Last Judgement's bench at `kak-v0.09`.

## 8. Milestones (each tagged)

1. **M1 — Campaign spine:** `CampaignDef`, `CampaignState`, the save, the Title → Campaign flow, fragments on the interlude, DP growth and bites, titles and endings as text screens. Night 2 is a three-card choice whose cards each lead to a placeholder ("Hold until dawn", 2:00) tagged with that card's path. Nights 1, 3 and 4 are the real missions. The whole arc is playable.
2. **M2 — The Gaze and Mira's House:** `GazeMeter` and its HUD; `VigilRoute`; the Faithful and their reports; Mira's House complete.
3. **M3 — Broken Lanterns:** the shrine structure, draining and relighting, guards, the Knights, the kneelers.
4. **M4 — The Vigil Flame:** Wren, the swap, `Searchlight` and phase 2.
5. **M5 — Story and balance:** Cael's lines, choice card text, ending panels, PixelLab portraits where wanted, and the balance pass of §7.

## Open items

- All names are placeholders the user may change. "Halcyon" is an ordinary English word, worth replacing with an invented name before release.
- All numbers are starting values, tuned in balance.
- Playable final missions for The New Faith and The False Lantern come after the prototype.
- Ruins and the dead carrying between nights, the holy city and capital maps, and Tier 4 are later work.
