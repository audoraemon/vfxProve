# KAK v0.11.1 "Playtest round 1": clearer screens, soldiers that hear the god, two ways to win every mission

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: for the user's approval. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-kak-v0111-playtest-1.md`.

Builds on v0.11 as landed (feat/Develop-Main `d539922`, tag `kak-v011-m6`; rulings in `docs/KAK_Version_0.11_Summary.md`
and `docs/KAK_v011_Rulings.md`; mission specs `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-0*-kak-v011-*.md` and
`2026-10-1*-kak-v011-*.md`). Decisions are numbered from 1 here: v0.11.1 has its own series.

The user's first playtest of v0.11 raised six points. This release answers each:

| # | What the user said | What changes | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Too much text on entering a mission; nothing says what to look at first | One objective line by default; the rest collapses behind TAB; the opening shows one thing at a time | §1, §2 |
| 2 | Text short by default; hide the overlay when off screen | Off-screen tags show a bare edge arrow; the name shows on screen, on hover, or with TAB | §3 |
| 3 | Too many symbols, no key for them | H opens a legend of every mark, with this night's own tags listed | §4 |
| 4 | Mind Whisper does not work on soldiers | Every control power reaches soldiers as it reaches citizens; one named hook is left for "strong ones" later | §6 |
| 5 | Some missions need one exact power | Every mission's main work can be done by two power families; the gate proves two loadouts win | §7 |
| 6 | The Tax Collector: a burnt counting-house should empty | When it burns or falls, everyone still inside runs for the Citadel; no collector is sent from it again | §5 |

## 0. What this release shares

- **Order:** the display work (§1-§4) first, then the Tax Collector (§5), then soldiers (§6) with one re-record of every moved
  reference, then the second routes (§7) a tier at a time, then the notes (§9).
- **Display is read-only.** §1-§4 change what the HUD draws and how the intro is paced, never the simulation. Scripted runs skip
  the intro and press no key, so no exact reference may move. A reference that moves in a display task is a bug in that task.
- **§6 and §7 move references on purpose, once each.** Each moved row is re-recorded in `tools/dev/refs/references.tsv` (or its
  tier file, Decision 34) in the commit that moves it, with the reason in the commit body.
- **The mission design rules hold** (memory `kak-mission-design-lessons`): no wait over 60 s, doing nothing loses, an acting
  player idles 45 s at most; chain pacing; opposition spread more than two Silent Doom radii; no luck; no hidden losses (a death
  that loses is tagged DO NOT KILL, an instant loss is warned with room to act, the winning strike never also loses); every moving
  target edge-arrowed; tags count down like the strip.
- **Objective clarity first:** where §1's collapsing and a rule above disagree, the rule wins (Decision 5: a hint that changes
  shows itself; Decision 9: the strip keeps its next countdown; Decision 12: arrows never go).
- **Keys in a mission today:** `1`-`6` slots, `Q`/`E` modes, `F` ascend, `R` restart, `Esc` cancel/pause, `Space`/`Enter`/left
  click skip the tour, `WASD`/arrows pan, `F3` frame meter, `F4` behaviour overlay, `F7` art toggle (from `mission.gd`,
  `targeting.gd`, `ui/*.gd`; `project.godot` has no input map). Free and chosen: **TAB** (details) and **H** (legend)
  (Decision 1).
- **Art:** text and drawn shapes only; no PixelLab.

## 1. A clear entry: the HUD collapses

Today a mission opens with the objective rows (or the scored panel with its stability legend), a three-line how-to-win plate, up
to three wish rows, the events strip, the status line, the intro banner, a tour caption and the prayers plate: often all at once.

### 1.1 Collapsed (the default)

`Hud.details` is false. The HUD draws:

- **The objective:** a mission without a score shows only its first objective row (the main one, e.g. "Kill the collectors 1/3").
  A scored mission (Last Judgement, Act III) shows its objective line, the stability bar with its percent, and the Escaped line;
  the five-name colour legend row goes (it is in the H legend, §4).
- **A cue:** a dim `TAB more` plate at the right end of the objective row (under the scored panel's Escaped line), as `_plate()`
  draws one, `UiTheme.COL_DIM`.
- **The how-to-win line:** hidden, except for `HINT_FLASH` seconds:
  - when play begins (the intro has landed), once;
  - each time its words change (a new phase of the director, a filled countdown changing words, the main objective done).
  - A countdown line whose only change is its number (`{n}`, MissionHints.filled()) does not re-flash on every second: the flash
    keys on `MissionHints.line(id, phase)`, the unfilled words (Decision 4).
- **The wishes:** one summary row in `Wish.COLOR`, "Wishes: K of N granted" (K granted of the N heard), none off the board.
- **The events strip:** the next timed event only (one row, not two).
- **The status line** (top right): only the alarm stage word, in its stage colour.
- **Everything else as today:** the clock, the Gaze bar, the rite bar, banners, Cael's lines, the ASCEND plate, the slots, the
  map's tags and arrows (§3).

### 1.2 Expanded

TAB toggles `Hud.details`. Expanded is today's HUD exactly: every objective row with its marks, the hint plate (wrapped,
`HINT_LINES`), every wish row, two strip rows, the full status line, the scored panel's legend row; the cue reads `TAB less`.
Expanded, the edge arrows carry their labels as today (Decision 12).

### 1.3 Rules

- `Hud.details` is a static var: it holds for the session across missions, restarts and acts. It starts false (Decision 2).
- The keepout (`stack_rect()`) and every `*_rect()` measure the plates **as drawn**, so collapsing shrinks the keepout and the
  tags spread into the freed corner. `_signature()` includes `details`, the flash, the summary row.
- TAB is handled in `Mission._unhandled_input()` beside F and R: pressed, not an echo, not in the ending; in the intro it is ignored.
- Nothing reads `Hud.details` but the HUD. It changes no rule, so no reference moves.

## 2. The opening, one thing at a time

Today the intro banner, the first tour caption and (on a board night) the "THE TOWN PRAYS..." plate are up together, over the
objective stack.

- **During the intro** (`Mission.in_intro()`), the HUD draws the clock, the slots and **one message**; the objective stack, the
  status line, the strip and the Gaze bar wait until play begins. `Hud.intro` (bool) is set by Mission while the intro runs.
- **The sequence:**
  1. The sweep in (the tour's first `MOVE_SECONDS`): the mission's intro banner alone.
  2. Each tour stop: its caption only while the camera **holds** there (`IntroTour.caption()` is "" while moving).
     `HOLD_SECONDS` rises from 1.6 to 2.4 so a caption can be read (Decision 6).
  3. On a board night that heard wishes: one last hold at the play spot, `PRAYERS_SECONDS` (3.0), showing only the prayers plate
     (`Hud.praying` is true only then). A sweep intro (no tour) gets the same hold after its sweep.
  4. Play begins: the clock starts and the how-to-win line flashes (§1.1).
- Space / Enter / left click still skips the whole intro, prayers included.
- Held banners raised during setup (THE TOWN PREPARES, SOLDIERS RALLY) queue behind the intro banner as today.
- The intro is longer by at most `0.8 × stops + 3.0` s of real time; the clock does not run during it, so no balance moves.
- FLOW's two intro checks (the town prays during the intro; Mira's House opens with a caption up) wait for the hold they look for.

## 3. Off-screen tags: the arrow alone

- **Decided by the user:** an off-screen tag (an edge arrow, `mode == "arrow"` in `Hud.tag_layout()`) draws no label plate.
- **Its label shows** when:
  - the tag is on screen (`mode == "map"`, unchanged);
  - the mouse is within `ARROW_HOVER_PX` (12 px) of the arrow's disc (`tip - dir * 5`) (Decision 11: cheap, the HUD already
    redraws the tag layer on change; the hover index joins `_tag_signature()`);
  - `Hud.details` is on (Decision 12).
- `tag_layout()` still places every arrow's label rect (so a hovered one draws where it would have), but an arrow's label only
  counts as **placed** (blocking later labels) when it is shown. So bare arrows no longer crowd map labels out.
- Two arrows under the mouse: the nearer shows its label; one only.
- Arrows never go: every moving target stays edge-arrowed (design rule).

## 4. The legend (H)

- **H** toggles a legend panel: page 1, then page 2, then closed. **Esc** closes it (and does not open the pause menu).
- **The night waits** while it is open: Mission freezes as the pause menu does (`Mission.set_frozen(true)`); the panel is a Control
  on the HUD layer with `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`, which takes H and Esc itself (Decision 13). R, F, slots and casts do nothing while
  it is open. It opens any time in a mission but the ending; in the intro it opens over the tour (the tour waits too).
- It is a `LegendPanel` (`src/game/ui/legend_panel.gd`), drawn with the HUD's own primitives (`Hud.mark_shape()`, `_draw_arrow`'s
  shape, `UiTheme.mark()`, the glyph rows of `DominionParts`/`DecreeParts`), centred, `UiTheme.COL_PANEL` behind, footer
  `H next   Esc close   the night waits`.

## 5. The Tax Collector: a burning counting-house empties

Today (`AssassinateDirector._indoors()`, the M2 controller's Task 2 fix ruling) a counting-house alight or fallen flushes out only
a collector hiding there or out on his round; those still **waiting** stay in and set out at their times. The user reverses it.

- **The rule:** the moment the counting-house is burning or destroyed, every collector still inside it, waiting ones included,
  bursts out in a fright and runs for the Citadel's gate. One banner: `THE COUNTING-HOUSE BURNS - THE COLLECTORS RUN`.
- **No collector is ever sent from a burnt building:** none is left WAITING, so no timeline event, chain or cast at its door sends
  anyone later; the strip's set-out rows drop (their condition is WAITING); no NEXT COLLECTOR tag.
- **How:** a flag on the base, `AssassinateDirector.flush_waiting := false`; `TaxCollectorDirector` sets it true. With it, a
  WAITING quarry in a burning or fallen hideout takes the same path as a hiding one today: `_come_out()`, state WALKING, `panic()`
  from the house; the next look alarms him, and with no hideout standing `_hide_goal()` is the Citadel's gate, so he flees there.
  The Bishop, The Informer and The Heart of Faith keep the base's rule (flag false; Decision 15).
- **Burst together, then their own way:** at once, as the user asked. `FLUSH_GAP` (s between one waiting collector's burst and the
  next) starts at 0.0; it is the first knob if loadout B's gate (§7) shows one strike at the door clearing all three: then 1.5,
  at most 3.0 (Decision 16).
- **The hint:** `tax_collector.hiding` becomes "Alarmed, he hides in the counting-house. Set it alight: every collector inside runs
  for the Citadel." `tax_collector.running` is unchanged.
- **The gate:** loadout A (Silent Doom) never burns the house, so the tax references should not move. The rule is proved by loadout
  B (Ember on the counting-house, Smite in the street; §7), and its gate is re-run with A's.

## 6. Soldiers hear the god

### 6.1 The rule

Every control power reaches a soldier as it reaches a citizen: Mind Whisper, Discord, Will-o'-Wisp and every other lure, a power's
fright (Collective Delusion's FALSE DANGER, Madness's frenzy), Collective Delusion's calm, Divine Congregation's gathering, Rewrite
Priority's orders. **Person no longer decides that a soldier is deaf.** Who a call reaches is the caller's choice; the town's own
routines keep reaching citizens only.

- **`Person.resists_mind() -> bool`**, false for everyone now: the one hook for the later "strong ones" (a future corps or role
  that answers true). Every mind-changing call below returns early when it is true (Decision 18).
- **`Person` drops its `soldier` guard** in: `panic()` (l. ~664), `observe()` (~687), `confuse()` (~729), `lure()` (~759),
  `reassure()` (~853), `whisper()` (~1046).
- **A soldier comes back to its post**, never into a citizen's day: wherever a lifted mind would hand a citizen back to its day
  (`_recover()` from a whisper's `_wake()`, Discord's `_come_to()`, a lure's or a look's end, a fright's `_settle()`), a soldier is
  sent to its post (`send_to_post(post)`, its anchor when it has none) after the same wait. A whispered soldier who was fleeing
  never flees (soldiers never flee). Directors that post their own soldiers (Knights, guards, watches) re-post them at their
  looks as they do today.
- **A soldier's lurable minds:** `POST` and `HOLD` besides the citizens' `LURABLE` (`RALLY`, `DUTY`, `FIGHT` stay deaf to a lure:
  a soldier running somewhere is not idle).

### 6.2 The power code

| File | Today | v0.11.1 |
|---|---|---|
| `src/fx/dominion/mind_whisper.gd` `pick()` | skips `p.soldier` | skips `p.resists_mind()` |
| `src/game/rules.gd` `cast()` whisper | `not soldier` | `not resists_mind()` |
| `src/game/targeting.gd` whisper aim | `not soldier` | `not resists_mind()` |
| `src/fx/control/will_o_wisp.gd` `drawn()` | skips soldiers | skips `resists_mind()`; soldiers at POST/HOLD come |
| `src/fx/quiet/discord.gd` `taken()` | skips soldiers ("soldiers keep their heads") | skips `resists_mind()` |
| `src/fx/dominion/collective_delusion.gd` calm | skips soldiers | skips `resists_mind()` |
| `src/game/crowd/crowd.gd` `fright()` | `_react()` over citizens | `_react(..., soldiers_too = true)`: a power's fright reaches soldiers too |
| `src/fx/dominion/divine_congregation.gd` `RESISTANCE` | soldier 1.0 (never comes) | no soldier entry (Decision 19) |
| `src/fx/dominion/rewrite_priority.gd` ESCAPE | soldiers skipped | a soldier is compelled to the nearest way out's mouth for the time, and never leaves the town (Decision 20) |
| `src/fx/death/death_mark.gd` the body's crowd | soldiers never come | they come (through `lure()`) |

### 6.3 What stays as it is (the town's own routines, not the god's)

- `Crowd._react()` for an ordinary cast's danger (`on_cast()`), the fright spreading, the sound reach, the danger watch: citizens
  only. Soldiers stand fast under a blast; the god must reach their minds on purpose.
- `flee()` and the evacuation, `regroup()` (City Emergency: a soldier has no home), fire crews, the rally: unchanged.
- `MadnessManager.RESISTANCE` soldier 0.8: a chance, not an immunity; unchanged.
- Divine Schism, Manufactured Hatred, Turncoat, Voice of God, Oathbound, Command Echo: already reach soldiers; unchanged.
- **Director holds are mechanics, not immunity:** the Sermon's rapt listeners, the Prisoner's bound gaolers, Market Panic's
  fearless wardens keep their holds. **The Armoury's armed men** are the garrison, soldiers in all but corps: they lose their
  deafness to a whisper (one whispered off his post leaves the target unsealed until he walks back, `RETURN_AFTER`) but stay past
  fear of blows, as every soldier does (Decision 21).

  walks back."; `armoury.guarded` → "Armed men (red) guard it: it takes no harm. Kill them, or whisper one off: he walks back.";
  `heart_of_faith.guarded` → "Lantern Knights (red) guard it: it takes no harm, its fire goes out. Kill them, or whisper, Discord or
  lure them off."
- Doc comments that state the old rule (BishopDirector's header, InformerDirector l. ~158, `discord.gd`'s "soldiers keep their

## 7. Two ways to win every mission

### 7.1 The rule

- **A power family** is what a power does (Decision 25). `PowerBook.FAMILY` maps each of the 38 keys to one of ten:

| Family | Powers |
|---|---|
| veil | doom, blight |
| death | deathmark, pestilence |
| strike | smite, heaven, tornado, tsunami, gravity, laser, orbital, judgement, glacial, solaris, nova |
| fire | ember, dragon, cinder |
| lead | whisper, oath, echo, congregation, priority, voice |
| draw | wisp, delusion |
| hold | discord |
| turn | madness, turncoat, hatred, verdict, schism |
| decree | noleave, belllies, magnify, abolition |
| passage | thorns, mirror |

- **Every mission has a main step** (the objective's own work) and a route for it in **two families**: loadout A's power and loadout
  B's power for it are of different families.
- **Loadout B** holds its main-step power and **not** A's main-step power, fits the mission's board slots and DP, draws from its
  board pool, and differs from loadout A. It uses the fifteen starting powers (DP ≤ 2) wherever it can; the three that need an
  unlock say so (Decision 26).
- **The gate proves both** (§8): the scripted player wins with loadout A (today's policy) and with loadout B (a new policy).

### 7.3 The new routes

Two shared hooks (plan Task 7) and five director changes (the tier tasks):

- **`Person.answers_lure`** (bool, false): a director sets it on one of its own people held on duty; `lure()` then takes him from
  `DUTY` too. While lured he walks to the light and stares (`OBSERVE`) as anyone does; the director reads a lure as the god's
  hand, as `EscortDirector` reads one already.
  - The Vigil Flame: Wren, from the moment he comes for the flame (rows 11).
  - The Relic Run: Pip, until he boards and again with the relic (row 20).
  - The Sermon: a listener who is not rapt (out of the preacher's VOICE, or the preacher silenced); one lured within
    `LURE_REACH` of the god's house goes in by Mira's rule (row 19).
  - A charge answers only a Wisp cast within `CHARGE_LURE_R` (3.0) of him, so a Wisp cast to draw bystanders elsewhere does not
    pull him off his road (Decision 28).
- **`MissionDirector.blight_target(at: Vector2) -> Vector2`** (virtual, `Vector2.INF` by default): a director's own thing Blight
  can ruin within `BlightFx.REACH` of `at`. `BlightFx` asks the director first; on a hit it rots there and calls
  `MissionDirector.blighted(at: Vector2)`, and the banner is `MissionDirector.blight_words(at)` in place of
  `Mission.blight_banner()`. Targeting's Blight aim rings the director's point as it outlines a structure.

  - The Lantern's Eye: an **unguarded** mirror's glass clouds and it counts as shattered, its wrights come out as after blows

  - A guarded one: Blight lands and does nothing but its banner `THEIR GUARD KEEPS IT` (no route around the opposition,
    Decision 29).
- **The Informer:** a contact confused by Discord after his visit, with no one else out of doors and not held within `SPEAK_SEEN`

## 8. References and gates

- **The gate** of a board mission (M2 ruling 55, unchanged), seeds 1-3: doing nothing loses 3/3; the scripted player wins ≥2/3; the
  median of its wins is 180-240 s; its longest idle is ≤45 s; no designed wait over 60 s.
- **Loadout B's gate** is the same for the 17 tier missions. For the 8 ★ missions (The Warning, Mira's House, Broken Lanterns, The
  Vigil Flame, The Festival, The Procession, Last Judgement, The Long Night), whose clocks predate the band, it is: wins ≥2/3 and
  idle ≤45 s on their board versions; the median is reported, not judged (Decision 33).
- **A second scripted loadout:** behaviour_check takes `--route=b` (not `--loadout=b`: `Mission._loadout()` already parses
  `--loadout=` as power keys and would warn on "b") (Decision 31). With it a scenario drafts `RouteBook.loadout_b(id)` and plays
  its B policy; `--case=play` as today. The ★ scenarios run B on the board version (`--board`); `feast --path=festival|procession
  --board` plays the board's Festival or Procession.
- **B references:** rows `<scenario>_b_play_<seed>` (seeds 1-3, 75 rows), which `refs_check.sh` already runs in parallel (its
  `_(none|play)_[0-9]+$` pattern). They live in `tools/dev/refs/routes_t<N>.tsv`, one per tier, which `refs_check.sh` reads
  beside `references.tsv`, so the tier tasks never edit one file together (Decision 34).
- **`tools/dev/gate.sh <scenario> [--route=b] [--board]`** runs the gate's six (or B's three) runs in parallel and prints one
  verdict line: `gate <scenario> route=<a|b> none_lost=K/3 wins=K/3 median=S idle_max=S PASS|FAIL` (Decision 35).
- **What moves when:** §1-§4: nothing. §5: nothing expected (A never burns the house); `tax_*` checked. §6: every row that casts a
  control power near a soldier (expected: the Warning's discord/mix cases, Mira's House, the Vigil Flame, lamb, prayers, ringers,
  informer, prisoner, hunt, sermon, relic, eye, exodus, heart; the digest and crowd_check cast none and must not move). §7: the new

## 10. Decisions

1. The details key is **TAB** and the legend key is **H**: both unused in a mission (TAB is used only on the board and Prepare,
   which are other screens), both one hand from WASD; F and R stay as they are.
2. `Hud.details` is a session-wide static, starting collapsed every launch; it is not saved (a first-time player always starts
   with the short screen).
3. Collapsed, a mission without a score shows its first objective row only: the other rows are loss conditions already shown by
   the clock, the strip, the Gaze bar or a banner.
4. The how-to-win line flashes for `HINT_FLASH` (8 s) when play begins and when its unfilled words change; a ticking `{n}`
   never re-flashes it.
5. A changing hint shows itself even collapsed: a new phase is a new instruction (objective clarity over brevity).
6. During the intro the HUD shows the clock, the slots and one message; captions show only while the camera holds; HOLD_SECONDS
   1.6 → 2.4; the prayers get their own 3.0 s hold at the end.
7. The intro's extra seconds are real time before the clock starts: no balance or reference moves.
8. The wishes collapse to one summary row, never to nothing (no hidden losses: a failed wish still shows in the count).
9. The strip keeps its next countdown collapsed (countdown tags rule); only its second row collapses.
10. The status line collapses to the alarm stage, the one figure that warns.
11. An off-screen tag's label shows on hover within 12 px of its arrow's disc: cheap (the tag layer already redraws on change).
12. Expanded (TAB), the arrows carry their labels as in v0.11: TAB means "all the text".
13. The legend freezes the night as the pause menu does and handles its own H and Esc; reading it never costs time.
14. The legend's "This night" column is built from the live tags, so it is always true for the mission being played.
15. The burning counting-house's full flush is The Tax Collector's alone (`flush_waiting`); the Bishop, the Informer and the Heart
   keep v0.11's rule.
16. `FLUSH_GAP` starts at 0 (the user's "at once") and is the first knob, to 3.0 s at most, if one strike at the door clears all.
17. The M2 controller's ruling "one fire never sets the whole night out at once" is reversed by the user for The Tax Collector.
18. `Person.resists_mind()` is the single hook for the later strong ones; false for everyone in v0.11.1.
19. Divine Congregation's soldier resistance (1.0, never comes) is removed; the clergy's and others' chances stay.
20. Rewrite Priority's ESCAPE on a soldier compels it to the nearest way out's mouth for the order's time; soldiers still never
   leave the town (escapes and stability count as before).
21. The Armoury's armed men hear a whisper like every soldier now, but stay past fear of blows; their hint says so.
22. The Informer's and the Heretic Hunt's quiet-door choices stay: a quiet door is still good design, and changing it would move
   their references for no gain.
23. The campaign is not retuned for §6; its references are re-recorded with the reason.
24. Policies before knobs: after §6 each gate miss is first met by the scripted policy doing what a player now would.
25. A power family is what a power does (ten families, §7.1), not its Authority: Dominion alone holds most control powers, and a
   whisper and a lure are different abilities to a player.
26. Loadout B uses the fifteen starting powers where it can, so a fresh god can play it; Broken Lanterns, The Crown's Bread, The
   Armoury and Last Judgement name an unlock.
27. Only the main step's A power is barred from B: helpers (a Doom on a bellkeeper) may repeat, so B stays a real loadout.
28. A director's charge answers a Wisp only within 3.0 of him, so loadout A's Wisps (cast to draw bystanders) keep their effect.
29. Blight never routes round opposition: a guarded standard or mirror only takes the banner.
30. The Warning's Death Mark is added to the board pool only; the campaign Night 1 and The Long Night's Omen keep theirs.
31. The second scripted loadout is `--route=b`, because `--loadout=` already means power keys.
32. The routes table is its own file (`RouteBook`), so five parallel tier tasks touch different lines of one small file.
33. B's gate on the ★ missions judges wins and idle only; their medians are reported.
34. B references live in one TSV per tier, read by `refs_check.sh` with `references.tsv`, so parallel tier tasks never conflict.
35. A `gate.sh` reads the gate's verdict from the runs: fifty gates (25 missions × A and B) are too many to read by hand.
