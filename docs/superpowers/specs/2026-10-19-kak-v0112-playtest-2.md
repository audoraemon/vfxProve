Date: 2026-10-19. Status: for the user's approval. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-kak-v0112-playtest-2.md`.

The user's second playtest raised four points. This release answers each:

## 0. Binding rules and what this release shares

### 0.1 The mission design rules (memory `kak-mission-design-lessons`; binding on every task)

- **No waiting.** Every timed wait is 60 s or less; doing nothing loses; an acting player idles 45 s at most; stages, not seals.
- **Chain pacing.** The next thing to do comes as the last one ends, never on a fixed far-off time alone.
- **Spread opposition.** Guards and escorts stand more than two Silent Doom radii apart (`SilentDoom.RADIUS` 0.8: more than 1.6 units),
  so one quiet cast never clears a whole guard.
- **No luck.** No outcome depends on street traffic or on a coin the player cannot see.
- **No hidden losses.** Every instant loss is warned plainly, with room to act; a death that loses is tagged DO NOT KILL; the winning
  strike never also loses. The Herald and the Lantern-Bearer carry countdown tags; the Lantern's speed is always on the clock.
- **Clarity.** Every moving target is edge-arrowed; tags count down like the strip; plates keep clear of the HUD stack, the strip and
  the slots.

### 0.2 What this release shares

- **Order.** Groundwork first (open powers, the mission clock's rate, the audio buses and sliders, the strike that lands), then one
  re-record and gate sweep of every mission for the awareness change, then the Lantern's shared mechanism, then each night adopts
  it, then the sound content, then the notes. The plan's task list is the binding order.
- **References move on purpose, once per cause.** §1, §2 and the Lantern's groundwork move none; §3 moves most scenario rows (one
  re-record, reasons in the commit body); each Lantern night's adoption moves only its own rows. §7 lists them.
- **Keys.** Unchanged in a mission (`1`-`6`, `Q`/`E`, `F`, `R`, `Esc`, `TAB`, `H`, panning, `F3`/`F4`/`F7`). The new Options panel
  takes the arrow keys and Esc only while it is open.
- **Art.** Text and drawn shapes only; no PixelLab. The Lantern's beam reuses the Searchlight's drawing.
- **Sound licence.** New sounds are built by `tools/audio/foley.py` from `C:\BURIN_NITRO\SoundLibrary`'s CC0 / public-domain packs
  (OpenGameArt CC0, Kenney, Wikimedia CC0/PD) and `tools/audio/synth.py`. **Never** Sonniss, never Stable Audio or other generated
  takes: the repo is public. `assets/audio/CREDITS.md` is rewritten from the recipes (`foley.py --credits`).

## 2. Sound

### 2.1 The mix and the sliders

Facts: `Music.DB` -10 (theme and three battle stems, `DUCK_DB` -12 under the pause menu) on no bus (Master); `Sfx` on bus `SFX`
(0 dB); `UiSound` on `SFX` too; `default_bus_layout.tres` has Master (compressor, limiter) and SFX only. No setting is saved anywhere:
`user://kak_save.cfg` holds progress only.

- **Buses.** `default_bus_layout.tres` gains `Music` and `UI` (each 0 dB, send Master). Music's players use `Music`; UiSound's use
  `UI`; Sfx, the crowd's panic bed and every power stay on `SFX`.
- **Music level.** `Music.DB` -10 → **-16** (the brief's start; first knob of §6.3).
- **Settings.** A new `Settings` (`src/game/settings.gd`) saved to its own file `user://kak_settings.cfg`, section `[audio]`, keys
  `music`, `sfx`, `ui` (0.0-1.0, default 1.0). **Ruling:** its own file, not the progress save: FLOW and the photo runs write test
  saves, and a volume must never ride along with a test save or be lost with a damaged one (Decision 4). Applied at boot by `Game`
  (`AudioServer.set_bus_volume_db(bus, linear_to_db(v))`, muted at 0); FLOW never writes it.
- **The Options panel** (`OptionsPanel`, `src/game/ui/options_panel.gd`): title `OPTIONS`; three bars of 10 steps, labelled `Music`,
  `Sounds`, `Interface`; footer `Click a bar or use Left/Right   Up/Down to choose   Esc back`. A change applies at once; closing
  saves. Changing Sounds plays one Smite crack (`hs_strike`, -12 dB) on the SFX bus; changing Interface plays `ui_click`; while the
  panel is open over a paused mission the pause duck is lifted so the music's level can be heard (Decision 5).
- **Where:** Pause gains `Options` (second: Resume, Options, Restart, Change powers, Missions/Campaign); the title gains `Options`
  (Campaign, Missions, Options, VFX Sandbox, Quit). **Ruling:** the title gets it too: the same panel, one more menu row, so cheap
  (Decision 6). Esc in the panel closes it back to its menu; it never resumes the mission.

### 2.2 A death you can hear

Today a death plays nothing; `Crowd._yelp()` cries out for the frightened only (`cit_yelp` -9.5, `cit_shout` -10, a 4-token budget).

- **Every kill** (`Crowd._on_killed()`, citizens and soldiers, every damage kind) asks a new `DeathVoices` (`src/audio/death_voices.gd`,
  pure logic, owned by Crowd) for a cue, and Crowd plays it positionally at the body (`sfx.play(cue, ground_pos)`):
  - loud kinds (every kind but the quiet ones): `cit_death` (citizens, 5 variants: a short cry and a fall) or `sol_death` (soldiers,
    3 variants: a grunt, mail and a fall) (Decision 7);
  - quiet kinds (`doom`, `deathmark`, `plague`): `cit_fall` (4 variants: a body slumping, no cry) for anyone -- an unseen death must
    not shout (Decision 8).
- **A mass kill is one wave.** Deaths within `WAVE_WINDOW` (0.25 s) of the wave's first are one wave: at most `WAVE_VOICES` (3) of
  them are voiced, nearest the camera first; a wave of `MASS_AT` (6) or more also plays one `crowd_death_wave` (a crunchy roar of a
  crowd falling, 2 variants) at the wave's centre. Across waves a token budget (`TOKENS` 4, refilled `REFILL` 3 a second) caps the
  rate; the Sfx pool caps each cue's voices (`voices`: 4, 3, 4, 2). Nova's 60 dead is one roar and three cries.
- **Nothing in the simulation changes:** DeathVoices draws no number from the crowd's `_rng` and changes no state the digest or any
  checksum reads; headless it only counts (`voiced`, `waves`).

### 2.4 Measures and the listening sheet

The controller cannot listen, so the release measures and the user listens:

- **`tools/audio/loudness.py`** (new; numpy/scipy, no new package): for every catalog WAV, integrated loudness (ITU-R BS.1770-4
  K-weighting; files under 0.4 s measured on 100 ms blocks) and true peak (4x oversampled), written to `tools/audio/loudness.tsv`
  (`cue  file  lufs  peak_dbtp`). `--check` exits 1 if a WAV differs from its row. **Played level** = the cue's `lufs` + its catalog
  `db` + the call's offset (a non-literal offset counts as its largest literal).
- **The floor test** (`tests/test_power_sounds.gd`) reads the TSV and every power's script: every PowerBook key's script plays at
  least one catalog cue at played level ≥ `QUIET_FLOOR`; every quiet key plays its own `q_<key>`; no two quiet keys' `q_` cues share
  a file; death and Lantern cues are within their targets (§6.3). `QUIET_FLOOR` = the loud powers' median main-cue played level - 6 LU,
  rounded down, fixed by the plan's measuring task (Decision 10).
- **The listening sheet** `tools/audio/ab/v0112/LISTEN.md`: one row per new or changed cue (file, LUFS, peak, what to listen for, a
  1-5 mark and a note column for the user); a `mix_<cue>.wav` beside each, rendered by `loudness.py --mix`: 4 s of `music_theme` at
  -16 dB with the cue at its played level at 1 s, so "does it read over the music" is one click; and six in-game checks (Title →
  Options moves each bar; Last Judgement with a Nova into the market: one wave, not sixty; The Tax Collector with Silent Doom on a
  collector: hear the cast and the fall; the Vigil Sandbox with each quiet power; a toll in The Warning: the flare; the pause duck).

## 3. Awareness: the strike lands before the town runs

### 3.1 Today (checked)

`Targeting._on_cast_made()` calls `Crowd.on_cast()` the moment a loud power is cast; it registers the threat at once and
`_react()`s: everyone within the danger's radius + `Person.THREAT_MARGIN` panics and runs, those within earshot look. Smite strikes
`SmiteFx.T_STRIKE` (0.35 s) later within `KILL_R` 0.6, so its target is already running. **Ruling (corrects the brief):** Ember is a
quiet power (`PowerBook` `"quiet": true`): it registers no danger at all, so §3 concerns the fourteen loud powers only; and Nova's
danger counts from its impact (`T_IMPACT` 2.3 s, the first kills), not its descent (1.5 s) (Decision 11).

### 3.2 The rule

- **A loud power's danger counts from its first impact.** `PowerBook.IMPACT := {key: seconds}` holds the first moment each loud
  power can kill, read from its effect script (the plan's test checks each against the script's own constants):

| Power | Impact (s) | From | Power | Impact (s) | From |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| smite | 0.35 | `T_STRIKE` | orbital | 1.45 | `T_SALVO_START + AIM_TIME` |
| heaven | 1.4 | `T_STRIKE` | cinder | 1.2 | `T_RISE` (the volcano burns at its foot) |
| tornado | 1.0 | `T_FORM` | judgement | 2.0 | `T_RISE` (the rise crushes within 1.3) |
| dragon | 1.2 | `T_ERUPT` | glacial | 1.2 | `T_ERUPT` |
| tsunami | 1.9 | `T_SWEEP` | solaris | 1.2 | `T_CHARGE` |
| gravity | 0.8 | `T_PULL` | schism | 1.8 | `T_BREAK` |
| laser | 2.0 | `T_WALK` | nova | 2.3 | `T_IMPACT` |

- **At the cast**, `Crowd.on_cast()` registers nothing and frightens no one: it holds the danger (its points, radius, reach, key) for
  `IMPACT[key]` seconds of the crowd's clock, and the calm citizens within the power's sight (`REACH[key][0]`) of a point **look**
  (`observe()` toward it): they stop and stare at the build-up.
- **At the impact**, the held danger is registered and `_react()`ed exactly as `on_cast()` does today (same radius, reach, severity,
  seconds, the same people), and `Crowd.danger_landed(key, points)` is emitted.
- **A key with no impact entry** (a scripted test's `""`, the sandbox, `crowd_check`) lands at once, as today.
- **Held dangers die with the act:** `Crowd.clear()` drops them; an act that ends before the impact never frightens anyone.

### 3.3 The startle

- **A frightened person freezes before running.** Whenever `Person.panic()` turns a person to PANIC from any other mind, they stand
  still for `startle_seconds()` -- then run as today. `startle_seconds()` lies in `STARTLE` (0.4, 0.8) s, fixed per person and
  deterministic: computed from the person's own `rng.seed` by a hash, never drawn from any generator (Decision 12). A person already
  in PANIC who is frightened again does not freeze again. Shelter-takers (`ShelterManager`) and those held by a Dominion or fearless
  are untouched (they never reach PANIC).
- It holds for citizens and soldiers alike, from every source of fright (a cast's danger, a seen Silent Doom, a collapse, a director's
  own `panic()` call, a lasting threat walked into).

### 3.4 Directors read the impact too

Directors that react to a loud cast as danger move to `Crowd.danger_landed`:

- `AssassinateDirector._on_cast()` (The Tax Collector, The Informer, The Procession's Prince, The Bishop): a walking quarry within
  `alarm_reach` of a loud power is alarmed at its impact, not its cast. The smoke-out at the hideout door stays at the cast (a noise
  at a door, not a danger).
- `FestivalDirector._on_cast()`: the goers a loud power breaks are judged at its impact.
- **Stay at the cast** (they are sight and noise, not danger): The Bell-Ringers' "THE ... POST SEES YOU", the Vigil Flame's and the
  Lantern's Eye's noise for their Searchlights, the Warning's "delayed by" bookkeeping, Heart of Faith's spare-win flag (Decision 14).

### 3.5 What does not change

Alarm escalation rates, the threat's severity and duration, quiet powers, soldiers' standing fast under an ordinary cast's danger,
the voices budget. The user chose "impact + startle", not a slower alarm.

**Ruling (corrects the brief):** The Omen also loses on the toll today (it uses `warning()`'s objectives), and The Sermon's copy
promises a bell that its code never rings into the Gaze; both join (Decisions 15, 16). Every other mission's toll stays as it is (the
town's alarm): the user's complaint was the instant loss, and spreading the Lantern to every night would rebalance 25 missions
(Decision 17). `MissionDef.lantern` (`""`, `"herald"`, `"watch"`, `"gaze"`) names the kind; `ActDef` inherits it; `TierBook` keeps it.

### 4.2 The toll

On the first `BellNetwork.rung` of a Lantern night:

- banner `THE BELL TOLLS - THE LANTERN TURNS` (in place of `THE BELL TOLLS - THE TOWN IS WARNED`); `lantern_flare` plays at the
  Temple;
- the Temple's Lantern flares and **one pale beam** lights from the Temple's spire (`TownLayout.TEMPLE`'s centre), a `Searchlight` of
  the Lantern's own with `hunt` on and `hold` `BEAM_HOLD` (6 s): it sweeps, and slides toward each loud cast and each death it hears;
- the town wakes as the bell already makes it (the alarm's jump, unchanged);
- the stage becomes **Stirring**.

The toll is latched: a bell unrung by The Bell Lies and rung again turns nothing a second time (Decision 18).

### 4.3 Stages and the clock's rate

| Stage | When | Clock rate | Beams |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asleep | before the toll | x1.0 | none |
| **Stirring** | from the toll | **x1.5** | one |
| **Burning** | a loud cast whose point a beam touches, or a death someone sees inside a beam | **x2.0** | two |
| Blind | the Lantern-Bearer dead | x1.0 | fade out |

Burning is the highest stage and never falls back to Stirring. Blind is final: the Lantern cannot turn again this act. A quiet cast
in a beam does nothing to the stage (Decision 19). Banners: `THE LANTERN BURNS - DAWN RUSHES`, `THE LANTERN IS BLIND - DAWN SLOWS`.

### 4.4 What hastens (mission time)

- **`Rules.clock_rate`** is the Lantern's rate (1.0 with no Lantern). The clock (`time_left`) runs at it; `Rules.mission_time()` is the
  act's seconds of mission time.
- **The night's schedule runs on mission time** (the brief's point 10, decided "yes"): every `EventTimeline` (its events, its
  `elapsed()`, the strip's countdowns, `seconds_to()`), and every time of night a Lantern night's director keeps by its own clock
  (Starfall's stars, a set-out, a wave, a cart's halt) -- anything the strip, a tag or the tour names as a time.
- **Real time stays real:** the player's cooldowns; every walk, climb, stare, look, retarget and beam sweep; the Gaze's per-second
  feeds; hit-stop. A director's reflexes are not its schedule (Decision 20).
- **Every countdown shown is in mission seconds**, so all of them tick at the clock's visible pace: the strip, a scheduled tag, and a
  walker's countdown (the Herald's, the Bearer's), which is his remaining walk in real seconds times the rate (Decision 21).

### 4.5 The Lantern-Bearer (the counterplay)

- `BEARER_AFTER` (10) mission seconds after the toll, banner `THE LANTERN-BEARER SETS OUT`: a priest (the living citizen of the clergy
  nearest the Temple's door, else the nearest citizen not reserved) carries the Lantern's flame from the Temple's door toward the bell
  tower's foot, walking (DUTY, slowed by `BEARER_SLOW`: about 21 s for the ~10 units), with **two Faithful escorts** walking in file
  behind him `ESCORT_GAP` (2.2) and twice that back -- more than two Silent Doom radii apart.
- **Tags:** the Bearer gold, edge-arrowed, `LANTERN-BEARER - AT THE TOWER IN 0:21` (counting down); each escort red, edge-arrowed,
  `ESCORT`; the bell tower an orange place tag `BELL TOWER` while he walks.
- **Kill him, any power** → Blind: the clock back to x1.0, the beams fade, `lantern_blind` plays, the stage is final.
- **Escorts take up the flame.** If an escort living within `ESCORT_SEE` (3.0) sees the Bearer fall (judged after a cast's other
  victims, as `Crowd.nearest_witness()` judges), that escort becomes the Bearer and walks on (banner `AN ESCORT TAKES UP THE FLAME`);
  only the escorts can, so at most two relays. **Ruling:** the brief gave the escorts no role; without one they are scenery and the
  spread rule means nothing. A relay makes them the opposition the design lessons ask for (kill or draw them off first, or strike all
  three), as the warning's and the report's relays already teach (Decision 22). Cost if wrong: a slower counterplay; the fallback is
  `ESCORT_RELAY` false (escorts as witnesses only).
- **At the tower** he keeps the vigil: banner `THE BEARER KEEPS THE VIGIL`, tag `LANTERN-BEARER - KEEPS THE VIGIL`; the speed-up
  stays, and he can still be killed there (the brief's preference), so the Lantern is never lost for good (Decision 23).
- **The strip** shows `The Lantern-Bearer sets out` before he leaves and `The Bearer reaches the bell tower` while he walks.

### 4.6 Herald nights: "Herald to the Temple"

On The Warning (campaign, the Omen, the board's Starfall) and The Bell-Ringers the toll does not lose:

- **Whoever rang it becomes the HERALD** and runs for the Temple's door: banner `THE HERALD RUNS FOR THE TEMPLE`. He first comes down
  the tower (`HERALD_DESCEND`, 5 s), then walks the ~10 units slowed by `HERALD_SLOW` (about 16 s): some 21 s of room in all
  (Decision 24). Tag gold, edge-arrowed, `HERALD - AT THE TEMPLE IN 0:14`; the Temple's door an orange place tag `TEMPLE`; the strip
  `The Herald reaches the Temple`.
- **The warning goes on with him.** A warning's errand is the bell until it tolls; after the toll (or once the bell is silenced) its
  carrier's goal is the Temple's door, at the Herald's pace. So the Herald is that warning's carrier: killed unseen, the warning dies
  (the objective counts it); killed seen, the nearest witness carries it on to the Temple (the warning's relay rule, unchanged). A
  later star's or post's runner, after the toll, runs straight for the Temple as a Herald too.
- **If the town's own bellkeeper rang it with no warning delivered** (a Local Emergency), he is a Herald of his own (a
  `TempleReport` at the Herald's pace, the same tag and relay), and the night is won only once he is stopped too.
- **A Herald reaching the door:** banner `THE LANTERN HEARS`; the night is lost, reason `lantern`.
- **A silenced bell** (cracked by Blight, its tower fallen) on a Herald night: the warning that called it runs for the Temple as a
  Herald, banner `THE BELL IS SILENT - THE WARNING RUNS FOR THE TEMPLE`; there was no toll, so the Lantern stays Asleep (Decision 13).
- **Winning:** a bell night is won only by stopping the warnings, and every Herald if the bell rang. The Lantern's stages, beams and
  Bearer apply as on every Lantern night.

### 4.7 Gaze nights

On Mira's House, The Vigil Flame, Broken Lanterns, The Relic Run and The Sermon the toll no longer fills the Gaze: it adds
`GAZE_TOLL` (40) once, and every rule of §4.2-§4.5 applies. The directors' own Searchlights (the Vigil Flame's and the Relic Run's
Phase 2) are untouched: the Lantern's pale beam is a separate light that costs no Gaze, only Burning (Decision 25).

## 6. Numbers and their tuning order

Tune in this order, one knob at a time, the gate re-run after each; at most three rounds per mission, else DONE_WITH_CONCERNS with

## 8. Gates

- **Board gate** (v0.11's, unchanged): seeds 1-3, doing nothing loses 3/3, the scripted player wins ≥2/3, median of wins 180-240 s
  (route a) / 150-270 s (route b, tier missions), longest idle ≤45 s, no designed wait over 60 s; the ★ missions judged on wins and
  idle (D33 of v0.11.1). `tools/dev/gate.sh` prints the verdict.
- **After §3:** all 25 missions × loadouts A and B re-gated (50 gates); a miss is met by the scripted policy first, the opposition
  last.
- **The toll case** (new, each of the eleven Lantern nights, seeds 1-3, `--case=toll`): the scripted player lets the bell toll (or
  forces it at a fixed time with `BellNetwork.call_keeper()` where the night never tolls by itself), then plays on: kills the Herald
  and/or the Bearer and wins ≥2/3. Its idle and median are reported, not judged (the policy waits for the toll on purpose)
  (Decision 32). Rows `<s>_toll_<n>` in one TSV per adoption task.
- **Per task:** focused tests, `bash tools/dev/run_suite_parallel.sh 8` (3 in a parallel phase), `refs_check.sh --only=...` for the
  touched rows, the touched missions' gates; FLOW alone where a task touches FLOW, the pause menu, the title, the campaign or a shared
  director.
- **Landing:** `bash tools/dev/land.sh --bench=kak-v0.11.1`: trial merge, suite, every reference, FLOW alone, the five-board bench

   nothing.
2. The tier's slots, DP and the unlock economy are untouched (the user's words).

4. Ruling: volumes live in `user://kak_settings.cfg`, not the progress save — FLOW and photo runs write test saves; a damaged save
   must not cost the volume — cost if wrong: a second small file.

   Veil sells — cost if wrong: the user may want cries there too (a one-line change in `DeathVoices.QUIET_KINDS`).
